    Last Updated: November 18th, 2026.

    Highlights

    • Stability and reliability improvements. 
    • New Feature Support
      • Adds support for ROCm 10.2
    • New Game Support
      • Crimson Desert Enhanced: Charting the Unknown DLC
      • Phantom Blade Zero
    • Fixed Issues
      • AMD Smart Access Memory may become disabled after driver installation on Windows® 10 systems.
      • Application crash may be observed on first launch of Resident Evil Requiem after resetting the shader cache on Radeon™ RX 7000 series graphics products and above.
      • Visual corruption may appear on character hair while playing Phantom Blade Zero on Radeon™ RX 9000 series graphics products. 
      • Excessive writes to the system drive may be observed while the system is idle on Radeon™ RX 5000 and RX 6000 series graphics products when using Windows® 10.
      • Intermittent driver timeout may be observed while playing War Thunder on Radeon™ RX 5000 series graphics products.
      • Application crash may be observed in some games after using the AMD Cleanup Utility on Windows® 10 systems with Radeon™ RX 9000 series graphics products.
      • System crash may be observed when launching an application from a long file path on Radeon™ RX 7000 series graphics products.
      • Intermittent application crash or driver timeout may be observed while streaming with OBS Studio on Radeon™ RX 9000 series graphics products.
      • A memory leak may be observed over time, with applications taking progressively longer to launch, on Radeon™ RX 5000 series graphics products and above.
      • Failure to launch PETAFAB: Bot Invasion may be observed on Radeon™ RX 6000 series graphics products.

    Known Issues

    • Intermittent application crash or driver timeout may be observed while playing Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced on Radeon™ RX 7000 series graphics products.
    • Intermittent application crash or driver timeout may be observed while playing Cyberpunk 2077 with AMD FSR Upscaling, AMD Radeon™ Anti-Lag and in-game frame generation enabled during video playback on a secondary display on some Radeon™ RX 9000 series graphics products. Users experiencing this issue are recommended to disable in-game frame generation.
    • Call of Duty®: Modern Warfare® Remastered may become unresponsive while using Alt+Tab during gameplay on Ryzen™ AI Max PRO 400 Series processors.
    • Black screen may be observed when launching Gears of War: Reloaded on AMD Ryzen™ AI 9 400 Series processors. Users experiencing this issue are recommended to switch the in-game display mode to Windowed and then back to Fullscreen.
    • Failure to install may be observed while installing AI Bundle components in some regions with limited access to HuggingFace and GitHub.
    • Black corruption may be observed while playing Call of Duty®: Modern Warfare® Remastered on Radeon™ RX 9000 series graphics products.
    • Intermittent blank or empty content may be observed in the tabs of AMD Software: Adrenalin Edition.
    • Failure to launch Crimson Desert may be observed on Radeon™ RX 6000 series graphics products. Users experiencing this issue are recommended to install AMD Software: Adrenalin Edition 26.11.1.

    Additional Information

    • When downgrading to a previous version of AMD Software: Adrenalin Edition, it is recommended to use the AMD Cleanup Utility.
